THE PLAYER JOURNEY — FROM PHONE TO STADIUM

The complete flow, exactly as a player experiences it. No passwords, no training, no manuals. If you can use WhatsApp, you can run a stadium.

LEVEL 1
📱

INSERT PHONE NUMBER

Sign in with phone number + OTP — the same way every app in India works. No passwords, no forms, ten seconds and you're in with your own player profile.

LEVEL 2
🛡️

BUILD YOUR SQUAD

Create your team once — name, logo, colors — and invite friends by phone number. The squad is saved forever. Every weekend, same team, one tap.

LEVEL 3
📅

BOOK THE TURF

Book a slot at a CricAdda turf. When your slot starts, the big screen greets your team by name. The stadium is yours for the hour.

LEVEL 4
🔑

ENTER THE CODE

The turf screen shows a 4-digit pairing code. Type it into the app and your phone becomes the remote control for the entire turf — teams, toss, themes, everything.

LEVEL 5
🏏

PLAY THE MATCH

Score ball-by-ball with big fast buttons. Every tap hits the big screen in under a second. Sixes explode into full-screen celebrations with stadium sound. Fifties get the player's name in lights.

LEVEL 6
⚡

AUTO-DETECT MAGIC

Boundary sensors and AI cameras detect 4s, 6s and runs by themselves. The scorer just confirms with one tap — and can override anything. Hardware detects, human is always the boss.

LEVEL 7
🏆

CLAIM THE LOOT

Match ends: result, full scorecards and Player of the Match on the big screen. Everyone gets the scorecard + auto-cut highlight clips on WhatsApp. Every ball you ever play builds your career stats and city leaderboard rank.

BOSS LEVEL
👑

TOURNAMENTS

Leagues and knockouts with auto fixtures and live points tables on the screen. Spectators at home follow ball-by-ball with no app and no login — just the match code.

The turf screen and the phone, side by side in your browser. Pair them, score a match, fire the boundary sensor — everything you just read, playable in 60 seconds.

  1. Pair — the screen shows a 4-digit code; type it on the phone. That's exactly how a real turf works: your phone becomes the remote.
