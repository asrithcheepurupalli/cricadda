# Case Study — Bringing the Stadium Experience to Turf Cricket

*A projected case study for a CricAdda pilot deployment. Figures marked (est.) are
targets/estimates to validate during the pilot, based on typical turf economics.*

---

## Background

**"GreenPitch Arena"** (representative pilot profile) is a box-cricket turf in a Tier-1
Indian city: one 100×50 ft caged turf, floodlights, bookable in 60-minute slots from
6 AM to midnight. Weekend slots sell out; weekday daytime occupancy is low. Rates:
₹800–1,200/hour (est., varies by city and slot).

### The Problems

1. **Commodity experience.** Six competing turfs within 4 km offer the same thing: turf,
   nets, lights. Customers pick on price and distance alone.
2. **Scoring friction & disputes.** Matches are scored on paper or one player's phone
   app. Scores get disputed, the scorer misses balls while playing, and nothing is
   preserved — players walk away with no record.
3. **No reason to return.** No stats, no rivalry history, no progression. A booking is a
   transaction, not a relationship.
4. **Single revenue stream.** The turf earns slot fees only — no sponsorship, no events,
   no premium products.

## The CricAdda Deployment

| Stage | What was installed |
|---|---|
| Month 1 | 8×5 ft outdoor LED screen behind the bowler's-end net + Turf Hub + speakers; CricAdda app rollout to regulars |
| Month 3 | Experience layer: themes, animations, tournaments, leaderboards, spectator QR, sponsor ad slots |
| Month 5 | Automation kit: boundary sensors + overhead & crease cameras; hybrid scoring, replays, auto-highlights |

**How a match works now:** the booked team walks in, the screen greets them by name. A
player pairs their phone with the code on screen, picks the saved teams, and the match
starts. Every ball scored on the phone appears on the big screen instantly; fours from
the boundary sensors and sixes from the camera arrive as one-tap confirmations. A six
triggers a stadium-sound celebration with the batsman's name. After the match, everyone
gets the scorecard and a highlights reel on WhatsApp.

## Outcomes — Pilot Targets (est., to be validated)

### For players
- **Zero scoring disputes** — every ball logged with author and timestamp.
- **Career stats & leaderboards** drive rivalry: teams rebook weekly "to defend the
  leaderboard."
- Highlights shared on WhatsApp/Instagram become **organic marketing** — every clip
  carries the turf's branding.

### For the turf (targets)
| Metric | Before | Target after 6 months |
|---|---|---|
| Weekday occupancy | ~40% | ~55–60% (leagues + leaderboard nights) |
| Repeat-booking rate | ~30% | ~50%+ |
| Avg. revenue per slot | slot fee only | +₹150–300 via premium "stadium mode" slots |
| New revenue streams | 0 | Sponsor ads on screen; hosted tournaments; highlight branding |
| Marketing spend | paid ads | Largely replaced by player-shared highlight clips |

### The tournament effect
One hosted weekend tournament (16 teams, entry-fee model, sponsored by a local sports
retailer with between-overs screen ads) is projected to generate more profit than a
typical full week of regular slot bookings — while filling otherwise-unsold weekday
evening slots with league fixtures.

## Why It Works

1. **The screen is marketing that pays rent.** It differentiates the turf on booking day
   and sells sponsor ads all day.
2. **Stats create lock-in.** Players' career records live at CricAdda turfs — switching
   turfs means abandoning your average.
3. **Automation is a premium, not a prerequisite.** The turf monetized the screen-only
   tier for months before investing in sensors, then priced "full stadium mode" slots
   higher.
4. **Every match produces shareable content** — the growth loop is built into the
   product.

## Lessons for Rollout

- Screen placement needs a per-turf survey (sight lines, ball-impact protection, glare).
- Boundary **sensors** should lead automation (cheap, reliable); cameras follow for 6s,
  runs and replays.
- Onboard the turf's regular teams personally in week 1 — the first 10 teams seed the
  leaderboard and evangelize the rest.
- Sell the first sponsor slot before the automation phase; ad revenue funds the hardware
  upgrade.
