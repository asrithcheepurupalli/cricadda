## 1. Executive Summary

Turf cricket is booming, but every turf offers the same commodity: a caged pitch and
floodlights. **CricAdda transforms a turf into a stadium** — a large LED screen displays
live scores, player names and celebrations; players control everything from their phones;
and sensors with AI cameras automatically detect fours, sixes and runs.

The result: a turf that players talk about, come back to, and pay a premium for — plus
entirely new revenue from sponsorships and tournaments.

## 2. The Problem

- Matches are scored on paper or a single phone — error-prone, disputed, and forgotten.
- Players get no record of their performance, no stats, no highlights, no reason to
  prefer one turf over another.
- Turf owners compete only on price and location, with a single revenue stream (slot fees).

## 3. The Solution

### The Stadium Screen
A large outdoor LED screen installed behind the bowler's end (optionally both ends)
shows the live scoreboard, batsman/bowler names, over progress, target and required
rate, celebration animations, instant replays, and sponsor content between overs.

### Phone-First Control
- Players sign in with **phone number + OTP** — no passwords.
- Teams are created once and **saved forever**; players are invited by phone number.
- The screen displays a short **pairing code**; entering it in the app turns any phone
  into the match controller — teams, toss, scoring, themes, replays, everything.

### Hybrid Scoring: Manual + Automatic
| Mode | Description |
|---|---|
| Manual | Fast ball-by-ball scoring pad on the phone — works on day one, zero extra hardware |
| Assisted | Boundary sensors and AI cameras detect 4s, 6s and runs; the scorer confirms with one tap |
| Automatic | The system scores boundaries and runs itself; the scorer only enters wickets and can override anything |

Manual override always wins, and every scoring event is logged with who entered it and
when — **no more disputed scores, ever.**

### The Experience Layer
- Six-hitting celebrations with stadium sound, milestone graphics, last-over hype mode
- Instant replays pushed to the big screen; auto-generated highlight clips shared to
  every player on WhatsApp after the match
- Career stats, leaderboards, and full tournament mode with fixtures and points tables
- Spectator QR: friends and family follow ball-by-ball live on the web, no app needed

## 7. Reliability, Answered Upfront

- **Internet down?** The system runs on the turf's local network; matches never stop,
  cloud sync resumes automatically.
- **Ball hits the screen?** Placement survey + polycarbonate/mesh protection per turf.
- **Auto-detection wrong?** Two-tap correction from the phone; corrections propagate
  everywhere instantly. Manual always wins.
- **Scorer walks off?** Control hands off to any other paired phone in one tap.
