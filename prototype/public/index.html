Turn any cricket turf into a stadium — big-screen scoreboard, phone control, auto-detection.

Open on a laptop / TV. This is the LED screen at the turf. It shows the pairing code.

Sensor Simulator

Pretends to be the boundary sensor & AI camera — demo the auto-detection flow.

Try it in 60 seconds

1
Open the Big Screen

On this computer (or a TV), open Big Screen. A 4-digit pairing code appears — exactly what the LED screen at a real turf would show.

2
Pair your phone

On a phone on the same Wi-Fi, open this page's address, tap Phone Controller, enter the code. Your phone is now the remote for the screen.

3
Start the match

Enter team names & players (defaults are pre-filled), pick overs, and start. The big screen flips to a live stadium scoreboard instantly.

4
Score & celebrate

Tap runs on the phone — the screen updates in real time. Hit 4 or 6 and watch the full-screen celebration (enable sound on the screen!).

5
See the magic: auto-detection

Open the Sensor Simulator on any device and fire a "boundary hit". The scorer's phone gets a ⚡ auto-detected banner — one tap to confirm, and it's scored. That's hybrid scoring: hardware detects, human confirms, manual always wins.

How the pieces connect

What this demo proves

✓
The pairing model works

Screen shows a code → phone takes control. No logins needed at the turf, no training, no keyboard.

✓
Real-time feels instant

Every tap reflects on the big screen in well under a second over local Wi-Fi.

✓
Hybrid scoring is the right UX

Auto-detections arrive as one-tap confirmations; a blue ring marks sensor-scored balls; undo fixes anything. Manual always wins.

✓
Event-sourced engine

Every ball is a logged event and the scoreboard is recomputed from the log — which is exactly why undo, corrections and dispute-proof scoring are trivial. Same architecture scales to production.
